# Episode 1 — Rain Station
Lead: Lin Xia

## Scene 1 — Rain Station / night
The last train is delayed while Lin Xia waits under the station canopy.

### Shot 1 — wide
Visual: Rain streaks the empty platform as Lin Xia checks the station clock.
Dialogue: Lin Xia: The last train should have arrived.

### Shot 2 — medium
Visual: Lin Xia turns toward a flickering sign and hears a distant bell.
Dialogue: Lin Xia: Someone is still here.

## Scene 2 — Station Exit / night
The rain softens as the station doors open.

### Shot 3 — close
Visual: Lin Xia finds a sealed letter beside the exit light.
Dialogue: Lin Xia: This is for me.
